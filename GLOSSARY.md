# CrapCard

CrapCard is a spaced repetition app in which authored content produces cards
that are scheduled and reviewed independently.

## Language

**Note**:
The content a user authors. A note produces one or more cards.
_Avoid_: Flashcard when referring to authored content

**Card**:
One independently scheduled question and answer produced by a note. A card
has its own review history.
_Avoid_: Note when referring to a scheduled question

**Note type**:
The form of a note that determines which cards it produces and what each card
shows.

**Card template**:
The named question and answer variant a note produces, such as its forward or
reverse card or one cloze deletion.

**Deck**:
A collection of notes whose cards are studied together.

**Review**:
A user's grading of a card during study, which affects when that card is due
again.

**Suspended card**:
A card the user has taken out of the study queue until they resume it.

**Buried card**:
A card temporarily hidden from the study queue until a chosen later day.

**Flagged card**:
A card marked for attention without changing its place in the study queue.
